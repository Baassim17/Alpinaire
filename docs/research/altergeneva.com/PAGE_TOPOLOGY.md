# Alter Geneva Page Topology

1. Fixed header with menu button, centered logo, and `Your project` CTA.
2. Hero with `GENEVA` baseline, surtitle, H1, and full-height watch image.
3. Intro text block.
4. Keywords section with three stacked keyword rows and looping inline videos.
5. Full-bleed media section titled `Customization is a limitless form of expression`.
6. `Our work` section with three project cards.
7. Footer navigation, socials, legal copy, cookie trigger, and legal notice popup.

Interaction model by section:

- Header: click-driven overlay menu.
- Hero and intro: static.
- Keywords/media sections: time-driven autoplay video.
- Our work: data-driven client refresh after initial SSR render.
- Footer/legal/cookies: click-driven overlays.
