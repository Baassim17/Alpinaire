# Alter Geneva Behaviors

- Header uses an overlay menu triggered by the left-side `Menu` button.
- The menu opens full-screen and swaps the trigger label from `Menu` to `Close`.
- The page uses autoplaying muted looping videos in the keywords and craftsmanship sections.
- A cookie banner is fixed to the bottom edge and animates in/out.
- A legal notice modal can be opened from the footer and dismissed with a close button.
- The home page requests `https://api.altergeneva.com/api/random` after load to refresh the three project cards shown in `Our work`.
- Responsive references captured:
  - Desktop: `1440px`
  - Tablet: `768px`
  - Mobile: `390px`
